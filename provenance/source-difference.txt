+For $p>3$ and $c\ne0$, the class of $\sigma_c=\zeta^{c\chi_p}$ on $H_p$
+is nonzero despite its trivial restrictions to every cyclic subgroup.
+Indeed its centre has the invertible central object of degree

+$\sigma_c$ were a coboundary, the identity-on-degrees tensor gauge to

+this degree and self-braiding. In the untwisted centre, a central invertible
+over $Z$ has half-braiding a character $\lambda:H_p\to\C^\times$, so its
+self-braiding is $\lambda(Z)=1$, a contradiction.
